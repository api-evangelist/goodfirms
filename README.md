# GoodFirms

GoodFirms (https://www.goodfirms.co/) is a B2B review and rating platform dedicated to identifying and showcasing top-performing software companies and IT service providers worldwide. The platform serves over 2 million monthly users seeking trusted technology partners, hosting 80,000+ verified client reviews across 110,000+ listed companies and software products spanning 400+ categories including web development, mobile apps, UI/UX design, cybersecurity, and IT management.

GoodFirms provides a 360-degree view of every company through verified client reviews, portfolio highlights, service focus, and pricing details. All company listings are verified for authenticity and regularly updated. The platform does not manipulate ratings or reviews, making it a trusted source for B2B buying decisions in the technology sector.

For companies listed on the platform, GoodFirms offers embeddable GoodBox widgets and Featured Badges that allow businesses to display their verified ratings and reviews directly on their own websites.
